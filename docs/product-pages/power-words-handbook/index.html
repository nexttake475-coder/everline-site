Phrases to Replace

Choose stronger language only when it truthfully reflects your role.

Responsible for X and YDirected X and executed Y — if you owned both
Helped the teamContributed to a measurable team outcome
Worked on a projectDelivered a defined project milestone
Tasked with improving AImproved A by a verified amount
Dealt with customer issuesResolved customer issues and documented outcomes
